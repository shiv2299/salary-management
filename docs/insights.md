# Insights: Which Pay Questions, and Why

Each Insights card answers one question the HR Manager is asked, or asks themselves before acting. A question made the list only if two things hold: it leads to a decision someone makes regularly, and the data the app already keeps can answer it. Cards that cross countries answer in USD so the countries can be compared (FR-7). Level bands show one country at a time, in its own currency.

| Question | Who asks | Decision it drives |
|---|---|---|
| How is payroll changing? (FR-14, FR-9) | Finance and leadership, at budget time | Next year's payroll budget, and which countries or departments are driving it. |
| What added to payroll in the last 12 months? (FR-15) | Finance, when payroll grew more than planned | Whether to slow hiring or slow pay. The two levers have different owners. |
| What are we giving in raises? (FR-16) | The HR Manager and leadership, before an increment cycle | The percentage for the next cycle (applied as a bulk revision, FR-4), and whether a country or department has fallen behind. |
| Do levels mean consistent pay? (FR-17, FR-6) | The HR Manager and hiring managers, at an offer or a promotion | What to offer at a level, how big a promotion step should be, and whether overlapping levels need re-banding. |
| Who needs attention? (FR-18, FR-8) | The HR Manager and line managers, before a review cycle | Who gets an out-of-cycle adjustment or a retention conversation: people paid outside their peer range, people with no raise in two years, and long-tenured staff paid less than new hires. |
| What changed recently? (FR-10) | Payroll, managers, and employees asking about their own change | Whether a change went through as agreed, and what to correct before payroll runs. |

Considered and dropped:

- **Are we paying at market?** It needs external salary benchmarks per country and role, which the app doesn't hold. Peer ranges and level bands answer the internal half: are we consistent with ourselves.
- **Is there a gender or other demographic pay gap?** It needs sensitive personal data and a policy on who may see it (`docs/requirements.md` §4).
- **Does pay follow performance?** There are no performance ratings in the data, and a rating system is a separate product.
- **Are leavers paid less than people who stay?** The app records when someone left but not why. Without exit reasons, a pay gap between leavers and stayers doesn't show that pay drove them out. The attention list already finds the stalled and compressed pay that would.
